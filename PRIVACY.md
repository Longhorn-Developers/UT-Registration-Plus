# UT Registration Plus Privacy Policy

**Effective date** November 25th, 2026

Longhorn Developers maintains UT Registration Plus, a browser extension that helps students plan courses at The University of Texas at Austin.

## Course planning and browser storage

The extension reads course information on supported UT-related pages to show course details, check schedule conflicts, and import classes from your registered schedule when you choose. It uses your existing UT browser session to retrieve information from UT. You sign in directly on UT’s website.

Saved courses, schedules, and custom calendar blocks are stored in your browser. Your browser may sync some extension preferences across devices if you have browser sync enabled. You choose where to save or share a schedule you export.

## Error and performance reporting

We use Sentry to identify bugs and performance problems. Reports may include error messages, technical details about your browser and the extension, performance measurements, page URLs and related page details, and a randomly generated installation identifier that helps link recurring errors.

- **Chrome:** The extension sends error reports and sampled performance data automatically, unless **Error and Performance Reporting** is turned off in UTRP settings. Course planning remains available with reporting off.
- **Firefox:** The extension sends reports only when **Error and Performance Reporting** is on and you have granted the requested Firefox permissions for technical and interaction data, browsing activity, and website content. Turn reporting off in the extension’s advanced settings to stop future reports. Course planning remains available with reporting off.

## Feedback

The feedback form requires a message and an email address on both Chrome and Firefox. When you submit the form, Sentry receives both so we can investigate your report and follow up if needed. On Firefox, you must also grant a separate browser permission before the extension sends your feedback.

## External services and data use

The extension retrieves course information from UT Austin’s course catalog. To display public information about the people who contribute to UT Registration Plus, it retrieves contributor names and statistics from GitHub’s API or through CachedAPI. These services receive standard connection information, such as your IP address. External sites you open from the extension handle your visit under their own privacy policies.

We use extension data for the features, troubleshooting, and support described above. Our use of data obtained through Chrome extension APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

On Firefox, our data collection and transmission practices are subject to and follow [Mozilla’s Add-on Policies](https://extensionworkshop.com/documentation/publish/add-on-policies/#data-collection-and-transmission-disclosure-and-control).

## Retention and your choices

You can delete saved courses and schedules in the extension or uninstall it to remove its local data from that browser. Exported files and shared copies remain wherever you saved or sent them.

We retain diagnostic reports for 90 days (need to confirm retention period) and submitted feedback for 90 days (needs confirmation). For privacy questions or requests to access or delete information you submitted, contact Longhorn Developers at contact@longhorns.dev.
